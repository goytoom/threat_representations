Why the rerun was needed

The three steering experiments in Section D.3 did not use the same evaluation scenarios. The hostility-vector experiment used the 20 held-out intergroup scenarios listed in configs/hostile_config.json. The two threat-vector experiments used an earlier set of 25 scenarios with <Group 1> and <Group 2> placeholders. That set no longer exists in the repository. The difference explains the two sample sizes in Table D46 (n = 100 against n = 125) and was the point raised in review.

Realistic-threat vector25 earlier scenarios12520 held-out (hostile_config.json)100
Symbolic-threat vector25 earlier scenarios12520 held-out (hostile_config.json)100

In the pipeline, n per steering condition equals scenarios × steered layers. Each scenario is generated once at each of the five selected layers and each α. The Zoom note of “20 scenarios with 5 rollouts” corresponds to these five layers.

What was run

Only the two threat-vector experiments were rerun. The vectors, the model, the decoding settings and the steering strengths are the ones used in the original runs. Two things were held fixed on purpose: the five steered layers were taken from the original runs instead of being re-selected, so that the scenario set is the only change, and the GPT-4o rating prompt is the current repository version, which rates on the 1 to 5 scale printed in SI Section D.1.2.

Caveats

  • Two things changed at once in the rating. The scenarios changed, and the rating prompt moved from the 0 to 4 version (shifted by +1 afterwards) to the 1 to 5 version. The baseline (α = 0) rose from about 1.3 to about 1.7, which matches the hostility experiment’s baseline of 1.63 on the same scenarios. The +2 versus 0 effect sizes are nearly unchanged, so the conclusion does not depend on the prompt version.
  • Layers were fixed, not re-selected. Re-running the layer-selection step would sample different layers. Fixing them isolates the scenario change. A fresh selection would be a different, slightly stronger robustness check.
  • Single-sample design. Each scenario × layer × α cell holds one generated response, as in the original runs. The per-layer table shows the ordering holds in every layer, but the per-layer means rest on 20 responses each.
  • Environment. The runs used transformers 5.5.4 instead of 4.48. A smoke test confirmed identical model loading, steering hooks and rating before the full runs.
